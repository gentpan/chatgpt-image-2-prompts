## 中文

```
为虚构乳制品品牌 LUNERA 创作一张高端竖版食品广告海报，以更国际化和旗舰级 Transit 方向精炼呈现。精确保留核心视觉逻辑：强烈的垂直分割背景，左侧为饱和深红色区域，右侧为光滑浅胡桃木棕色桌面区域，从干净的俯视到略微倾斜的平铺视角查看。一片主角吐司配雕塑感黄油卷和一个高级包装黄油块必须保持中心商业故事，由一把银黄油刀、几块黄油立方体和几颗克制的坚果碎片支撑。整个海报必须感觉比标准快消品布局更干净、更编辑化、更全球高端、更受控。

主产品故事：在中右区域附近放置一片金黄吐司，顶部覆盖几条优雅卷曲的淡奶油色咸黄油丝带。黄油卷必须是主要食欲触发点：光滑、雕塑感、柔和发光，具有可信的厚度、奶油密度和精致的高光衰减。在吐司附近的右下区域，放置一个高级包装黄油块，略微倾斜，带有奶油和红色的干净原创虚构标签设计，高度易读但低调。在左侧，放置一把银色黄油刀向内延伸，刀刃上有柔软的一坨黄油,朝向吐司创造微妙的对角关系。只散布极少量黄油立方体和坚果碎片，以保持构图空灵和高端。

食物写实性：以卓越的写实性和克制渲染黄油：淡暖黄色调、柔软可涂抹质感、清晰切割边缘、缎面奶油感、轻柔光泽和美丽的卷曲结构。吐司必须展示精致多孔面包芯、酥脆褐色边缘和精致面包店写实感，无过度纹理。包装黄油块应感觉厚实高端，具有可信的包装折痕、精确阴影和优雅印刷效果。小黄油立方体应感觉新鲜、略微软化和整洁构成。

排版和品牌：使用完全原创的高端排版系统。在左上或中上附近放置小品牌标记和简短支持行。在左侧红色区域添加一个粗体英文标题块，使用原创文案如"SLOW CRAFT. PURE INDULGENCE."或"LAYERED SOFTNESS. DAILY LUXURY."。在左下附近添加一个紧凑支持行，在右上区域附近添加一个小品牌声明。所有文案必须原创、稀疏且高度受控。排版应感觉更编辑化和奢华而非促销性，具有更清洁的间距和更强的层次结构。

光照和氛围：使用柔和定向高级影棚照明，从右上方照射，赋予黄油卷精致高光、刀具微妙金属光泽、包装柔和维度感，以及红色和木质表面优雅的色调过渡。增加写实性和材质分离，但保持阴影干净、柔和且国际化感觉。整个海报应感觉精致、食欲丰富且自信高端。

风格：国际旗舰乳制品活动、高级快消食品品牌、编辑性商业海报、超逼真食品摄影、极简道具造型、奢华超市发布视觉、戛纳级产品层次。一切应感觉比本地零售广告更提炼和全球高端。

色彩系统：深红色、温暖胡桃木棕色、奶油淡黄油黄色、金黄吐司米色、银色刀具、克制的奶油红色包装点缀。保持调色板极简、大胆、优雅且高度令人难忘。

负面提示：无复制品牌名称、无复制口号、无杂乱道具、无到处乱飞的碎屑、无融化黄油混乱、无低细节吐司纹理、无扭曲包装、无浑浊阴影、无黑色污点、无脏桌面、无廉价超市样机外观、无水印、无现有标志。
```

## English

```
Create a premium vertical food advertising poster for a fictional dairy brand called LUNERA, refined in a more international and flagship Transit direction. Preserve the core visual logic exactly: a strong vertical split background with a saturated crimson-red field on the left and a smooth light walnut-brown tabletop field on the right, viewed from a clean top-down to slightly angled flat-lay perspective. One hero slice of toast with sculpted butter curls and one premium wrapped butter package must remain the central commercial story, supported by a silver butter knife, a few butter cubes, and a few restrained nut fragments. The whole poster must feel cleaner, more editorial, more globally premium, and more controlled than a standard FMCG layout.

Main product story:
Place one slice of golden toast near the center-right area, topped with several elegant curled ribbons of pale creamy salted butter. The butter curls must be the main appetite trigger: smooth, sculptural, softly luminous, with believable thickness, creamy density, and refined highlight rolloff. Near the toast, position one premium wrapped butter package in the lower-right area, angled slightly, with a clean original fictional label design in cream and red, highly legible but understated. On the left side, place a silver butter knife entering inward with a soft dollop of butter on the blade, creating a subtle diagonal relation toward the toast. Scatter only a very small number of butter cubes and nut fragments to keep the composition airy and premium.

Food realism:
Render the butter with exceptional realism and restraint: pale warm yellow tone, softly spreadable texture, crisp cut edges, satin creaminess, gentle sheen, and beautiful curl structure. The toast must show delicate porous crumb, crisp browned edges, and refined bakery realism without over-texturing. The wrapped butter block should feel substantial and premium, with believable wrap folds, precise shadowing, and elegant print finish. Small butter cubes should feel fresh, slightly softened, and neatly composed.

Typography and branding:
Use a fully original premium typography system. Place a small brand mark and short supporting line near the upper-left or upper-center. Add one bold English headline block on the left red field, using original copy such as "SLOW CRAFT. PURE INDULGENCE." or "LAYERED SOFTNESS. DAILY LUXURY." Add one compact supporting line near the lower-left and one small brand statement near the upper-right area. All copy must be original, sparse, and highly controlled. Typography should feel more editorial and luxurious than promotional, with cleaner spacing and stronger hierarchy.

Lighting and mood:
Use soft directional premium studio lighting from upper-right, giving the butter curls delicate highlights, the knife subtle metallic gleam, the package soft dimensionality, and the red and wood surfaces elegant tonal transitions. Increase realism and material separation, but keep the shadows clean, soft, and international in feel. The whole poster should feel polished, appetite-rich, and confidently premium.

Style:
International flagship dairy campaign, premium FMCG food branding, editorial commercial poster, hyper-real food photography, minimal prop styling, luxury supermarket launch visual, Cannes-level product hierarchy. Everything should feel more distilled and globally premium than a local retail ad.

Color system:
Deep crimson red, warm walnut brown, creamy pale butter yellow, golden toast beige, silver knife, restrained cream-and-red packaging accents. Keep the palette minimal, bold, elegant, and highly memorable.

Negative prompt:
no copied brand names, no copied slogan, no cluttered props, no messy crumbs everywhere, no melted butter chaos, no low-detail toast texture, no warped package, no muddy shadows, no black blotches, no dirty tabletop, no cheap supermarket mockup look, no watermark, no existing logos.
```
