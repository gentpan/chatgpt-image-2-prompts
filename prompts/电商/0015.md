Emphasize fashion-editorial realism: natural body proportions, soft window light from the left, gentle shadows, shallow depth of field, detailed fabric ribbing and trouser seams, realistic skin texture with tiny moles on the upper chest and faint subtle veins, tasteful and non-explicit. Composition should feel like a candid Japanese street-style or lifestyle fashion photo taken indoors, 3:4 vertical aspect ratio, neutral minimal background, warm tones, high-resolution DSLR look, no text, no watermark, no extra people or objects.
